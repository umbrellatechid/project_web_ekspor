Frequently Asked Question

How do I contact customer support?
Provide contact information for customer support, including phone numbers, email addresses, and any available chat support.
How early should I arrive before departure?
We recommend arriving at least 2 hours prior to scheduled departure for standard cargo handling, and 3 to 4 hours for international cross-border dispatches.
How do logistics providers manage supply chain risks?
Logistics providers manage risks through real-time GPS tracking, multi-modal backup routes, proactive customs compliance, and full transit cargo insurance.
What happens if my bus/train/plane is delayed?
Our 24/7 central dispatch automatically schedules alternate transportation routes and informs customers with continuous live status alerts.
